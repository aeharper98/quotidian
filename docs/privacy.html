Privacy Policy

Quotidian: Freestyle Rap · Last updated 21 November 2026

Quotidian is built so that your practice stays on your phone. We don't run accounts, we don't track you, and we don't sell anything about you.

What we collect

Nothing leaves your device. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What's stored on your phone

  • Your settings, such as beat, vocabulary level and reminder time.
  • Your word bank: the words you've looked up and how well you know them.
  • Your streak and session history.
  • Recordings you choose to make.

This data lives only in the app's storage on your device. If iCloud device backup is on, it's included in your Apple backup like any other app data. Deleting the app deletes it all. You can also clear it in Settings → Reset progress.

Microphone

Quotidian asks for the microphone only when you turn on recording. Recordings are saved on your device. They're never uploaded, and they're shared only if you use the share button yourself.

Notifications

If you allow it, the app schedules a daily reminder with that day's word. The reminder is created on your phone; we don't run a notification server.

Music from other apps

In "Your own music" mode, Quotidian lets other apps like Spotify keep playing. It doesn't access, read or record their audio. If you record in this mode, the microphone may pick up music playing from your speaker.

Children

Quotidian doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It collects nothing at all.

Future features

If we add features that need to send data off your device, such as AI feedback on a recording, they'll be opt-in. We'll update this policy and tell you in the app before anything is sent.
