Quotidian Support

Learn freestyle rap and new words, one tap at a time.

I can't hear the beat

Turn the volume up. Quotidian plays even with the silent switch on, but a Bluetooth speaker or headphones that disconnected can take the sound with them. Reconnect them and start the session again.

How do I use my own music?

Start your music in Spotify, Apple Music or any other app first. Then open Quotidian, choose "Your own music" and set the tempo with Tap tempo. Quotidian won't play anything, so your music keeps going.

Why do "karma" and "farmer" rhyme?

Said out loud, the r after a vowel goes quiet, so they sound the same at the end. Quotidian rhymes by sound, not spelling.

How does the word bank work?

Any word you tap during a session is saved. It comes back in later sessions, and once you've seen it 3 times without tapping it, it's marked as known.
